# Customer legal and trust readiness

This is a readiness tracker, not legal advice or a set of binding terms. Final customer-facing documents require appropriate legal review.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Terms of Service / SaaS Terms | Define the customer service relationship | TBD; legal drafting/review required |
| Privacy Policy | Explain relevant personal-data processing | TBD; must match implemented processing |
| Data Processing Agreement | Address controller/processor terms where applicable | TBD; legal drafting/review required |
| Subprocessor list | Disclose relevant selected vendors and changes | Framework exists; vendors TBD |
| Security/Privacy overview | Explain evidence-based technical and organizational posture | Engineering baseline exists; implementation pending |
| Retention/deletion information | Explain lifecycle and customer choices | Requirements exist; periods/workflows TBD |
| Support and security contact | Provide customer and vulnerability-reporting paths | TBD |
| Billing/cancellation/refund terms | Support paid plans if introduced | Out of current Beta scope; TBD before paid offering |

HubSpot Marketplace terms do **not** replace our own customer-facing provider terms, privacy information, or applicable data-processing commitments.

## Alignment rules

- Architecture and implementation must be capable of honoring every promise made in approved documents.
- Legal/trust artifacts must reflect actual data, vendors, regions, retention, deletion, security controls, support, and product behavior.
- Do not claim GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, certification, zero risk, or completed legal review without explicit evidence.
- Review legal/trust content whenever processing, providers, subprocessors, geography, retention, billing, onboarding, or security behavior changes.

The onboarding/terms-acceptance mechanism (including whether and how clickwrap is used), document versions, acceptance evidence, governing terms, launch details, and legal review owner are TBD. Decide the mechanism before external onboarding that requires acceptance; finalize applicable artifacts before they are presented to customers.
